Blind cave fish

Astyanax mexicanus

In caves in Mexico lives a small pink fish with no eyes at all. Its cousins in the rivers outside can see perfectly – but in total darkness, eyes are useless.

Did you know?

Baby cave fish start growing eyes, but the eyes never finish developing and disappear as the fish grows.

What makes it special

No eyes

Over many generations in the dark, the eyes disappeared.

Super sensitive skin

A line of sensors along its body feels the tiniest movements in the water.

Fat storage

It stores extra fat, so it can survive long periods with little food.

Hardly sleeps

Cave fish sleep much less than their river cousins.

Swim blind

Real blind cave fish feel the water around them with a line of sensors along their body. That is how they ‘see’ rocks without eyes.

Quick quiz

Cave fish and river fish with eyes are…

The same species! They can even have babies together.

How does the cave fish find its food?

It feels the water. Sensors along its body pick up tiny movements, like a sense of touch from a distance.

Do baby cave fish start with eyes?

Yes! The eyes start to form, then stop developing as the fish grows.
