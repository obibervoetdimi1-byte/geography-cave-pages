Why do they all look so pale?

Colour protects skin from sunlight and helps animals hide or show off. In a cave there is no sun and nobody can see you – so over thousands of generations, the colour simply fades away.

How to survive in the dark

No light

Eyes are useless without light. Many cave animals lose them and use touch, smell and vibrations instead.

No sun

Without sunlight there are no plants. Food has to drip, wash or fly in from the outside world.

Little food

To save energy, cave animals move slowly, grow slowly and can go a long time without eating.
